# Waná Guest Policies (template — replace with your operational policy)

## Compensation — minor cleaning issues
- Acknowledge the issue promptly and apologize for the inconvenience.
- For verified minor cleaning issues (e.g., missed surface wipe, linens not refreshed): offer a one-time credit of 10–15% of the affected night(s), capped at one night’s rate.
- Credits apply to a future Waná stay or are processed as a partial refund within 5–7 business days if no rebooking is planned.
- Escalate to operations if the guest reports health/safety concerns, pests, or repeated issues on the same property.

## Cancellation (moderate policy — default)

- **Pending payment:** cancel anytime in Mi cuenta — no charge.
- **Confirmed — 5+ days before check-in:** 100% refund to original payment method.
- **Confirmed — 2 to 4 days before check-in:** 50% refund.
- **Confirmed — less than 48 h before check-in:** no refund.
- **Checked in / completed:** contact support; cancellations handled case-by-case per `policies.txt` compensation rules.
- Refunds: Stripe automated when configured; Bold may require manual processing in dashboard (API marks `pending_manual`).

## Communication standards
- Respond within 24 hours during a stay; within 48 hours for post-stay issues.
- Never blame the guest; focus on resolution and next steps.
